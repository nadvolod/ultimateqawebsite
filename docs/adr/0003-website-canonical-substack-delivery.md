# Posts live on ultimateqa.com; Substack only delivers the Newsletter

The Newsletter (10–15k subscribers) moves from ConvertKit to Substack because Substack is free at that size and Kit's free plan stops at 10,000. Substack can't set canonical URLs, so full Posts are published only on ultimateqa.com and Newsletter issues summarise and link back, keeping search authority on our domain. Listmonk + Amazon SES was the alternative, cheaper to own, but rejected for setup time and the deliverability work it brings.
