# Posts live on ultimateqa.com; Substack only delivers the Newsletter

The Newsletter (10–15k subscribers) moves from ConvertKit, which was too expensive, to Substack, because Substack is free at that size; beehiiv was briefly chosen on 2026-11-01 and dropped the same day because its free plan stops at 2,500 subscribers per workspace. Substack can't set canonical URLs, so full Posts are published only on ultimateqa.com and Newsletter issues summarise and link back, keeping search authority on our domain. Listmonk + Amazon SES was the alternative, cheaper to own, but rejected for setup time and the deliverability work it brings.
