# Posts live on ultimateqa.com; beehiiv only delivers the Newsletter

The Newsletter (10–15k subscribers) moves from ConvertKit, which was too expensive, to beehiiv. Substack was the first choice because it is free at that size; on 2026-11-01 the owner chose beehiiv instead. Full Posts are published only on ultimateqa.com and Newsletter issues summarise and link back, keeping search authority on our domain. Listmonk + Amazon SES was the alternative, cheaper to own, but rejected for setup time and the deliverability work it brings.
