## Overview

UltimateQA partnered with an international health organization to build a comprehensive automated testing suite from scratch. The collaboration saved 8 hours of manual testing daily and delivered rapid feedback within 4 minutes.

<a id="h-the-challenge" />

### The Challenge

The international health organization faced a series of challenges that hindered its software development and testing processes:

-   Time-consuming manual testing was causing delays in software releases.
-   The lack of immediate feedback affected the development cycle’s quality and speed.
-   Absence of an existing automated testing infrastructure.
-   Comprehensive testing needs, including API, end-to-end, cross-browser, front-end performance, and accessibility tests.

<a id="h-the-solution" />

### The Solution

Drawing upon its extensive expertise in automated testing, UltimateQA proposed and executed a robust, multi-faceted solution:

-   **Tool Selection:** Employed state-of-the-art technologies like Playwright, TypeScript, Mulesoft, Azure DevOps, and GitHub Actions.
-   **Rapid Deployment:** Developed a suite of automated API, e2e, cross-browser, front-end performance, and accessibility tests.
-   **Time Efficiency:** Successfully reduced manual testing time by 8 hours daily.
-   **Quick Feedback:** Implemented a system that provides actionable feedback within 4 minutes.

<a id="h-the-results" />

### The Results

The collaboration between UltimateQA and the international health organization led to transformative outcomes:

-   **Time Savings:** The team will save 8 hours daily, allowing them to focus more on feature development and innovation.
-   **Rapid Feedback:** Developers receive feedback within 4 minutes, thus significantly accelerating the development cycle.
-   **Comprehensive Coverage:** The robust suite of automated tests ensures high software quality across multiple dimensions.
-   **Increased Confidence:** As a result, developers and business stakeholders have gained increased confidence in the software’s quality and reliability.
-   **Data-Driven Decisions:** The system’s real-time analytics and reporting capabilities enable more informed decision-making.

<a id="h-technologies-used" />

### Technologies Used

-   Playwright
-   TypeScript
-   Mulesoft
-   Azure DevOps
-   GitHub Actions

<a id="h-future-directions" />

### Future Directions

Ultimately, UltimateQA and the international health organization first aim to refine the automated testing suite, and following this, they plan to expand it even further. They are exploring additional technologies and methodologies to keep pace with the rapidly evolving needs of healthcare software.
