## How Does Thumbnail Wizard Work?

Operating under user-defined instructions, [Thumbnail Wizard](https://chat.openai.com/g/g-HqSbynBiR-thumbnail-wizard) simplifies the thumbnail creation process to an unprecedented degree. Upon receiving a video title or brief description, the AI identifies three key visual elements. These are essential to the video’s theme. They weave into a modern, sleek design, adhering to minimalism while maximizing impact. The wizard excludes text or letters from the design except for company logos. This ensures that the thumbnails are universally appealing. They rely on visual storytelling to attract clicks.
